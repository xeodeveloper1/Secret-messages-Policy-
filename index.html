1. Overview

Zikr & Dua (package name: com.helsondavid.zikranddua), developed by XEO Technology, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. The app is designed as a private, offline-first spiritual companion for Tasbih counting, Zikr logging, Qibla direction, and local prayer times.

Summary: We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal data to our servers or third parties. All your Zikr counts, custom presets, history logs, and settings remain locally on your device.

2. Information We Store Locally on Your Device

To provide persistent functionality across sessions, Zikr & Dua stores the following app data exclusively in local on-device storage (Android Room database and DataStore Preferences):

  • Tasbih & Zikr Data: Active count, target count, custom Zikr & Dua presets, and session history entries.
  • App Preferences: Night Mode theme preference, haptic feedback toggle, and tap sound effect toggle.
  • Location & Prayer Preferences: Your selected city or last chosen coordinates, timezone, preferred prayer calculation method, and any manual prayer-time minute adjustments.

No user account, login, email address, or cloud synchronization is required or used by this application.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — Optional): Requested only if you choose to tap the GPS / current location option in the Qibla or Prayer Times screen. Your approximate coordinates are processed locally on your device to calculate Qibla bearing, distance to Makkah, and daily prayer times, and may be resolved to a city label through Android's built-in Geocoder service. You may decline this permission and select a city manually instead.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used solely to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when tapping the Tasbih counter or reaching a target count, when enabled in app settings.

4. Device Sensors

When you open the Qibla tab, Zikr & Dua reads your device's built-in accelerometer and magnetometer sensors in real time to point the Qibla compass needle accurately. Sensor data is processed transiently in memory on your device and is never logged, stored, or shared.

5. Exporting & Sharing Your Zikr History

If you tap the Export or Share action in the History screen, the app formats your Zikr log into plain text locally and lets you either save it to a file location of your choice via Android's system file picker or share it via another app you explicitly select. Zikr & Dua does not request broad device storage permissions and cannot access other files on your device.

6. Third-Party Services, Ads & Analytics

Zikr & Dua does not include advertisements, behavioral tracking, analytics SDKs, or third-party data brokers. The app does not declare the Android INTERNET permission and does not operate a developer backend server.

If you choose to use the optional GPS location feature, Android's system location services (Google Play Services Fused Location Provider and Android Geocoder) are invoked by the operating system under their own platform privacy terms.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all app data resides solely on your device, you have complete control over it at all times:

  • Delete individual sessions or clear your entire Zikr history directly inside the History tab.
  • Reset custom prayer-time adjustments inside the Edit Prayer Times sheet.
  • Clear app data in your Android device Settings or uninstall the app to permanently remove all stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

Zikr & Dua does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted off the device, the app is safe for users of all ages.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

XEO Technology may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features or permissions are introduced in future versions of the application. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
